108 Practice Control Roomfor the Medreach integration PRACTICE – NOT THE REAL 108 Connecting…
What is this? It plays the role of the state's 108 / 102 dispatch system. When a hospital accepts a patient and the family asks for an ambulance, Medreach sends a signed incident here over HTTPS. This control room assigns the nearest suitable ambulance from its own fleet, drives it, and sends signed status updates back – exactly the contract the real 108 system would use. To try it: report an emergency on /report and choose Ambulance.

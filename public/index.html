Built for villages

Works even when the network doesn't

Many villages have only 2G, or no data at all. Medreach keeps helping at every level of signal – and never hides the call to 108.

  1. 4G / 3G

    Full app

    Voice in Hindi or English, AI understanding, live hospital status, acceptance before travel, 108 ambulance and live tracking.

  2. Weak 2G

    Lite + SMS

    Requests give up quickly instead of hanging. One SMS reaches the Medreach gateway, which asks the hospital, requests the 108 ambulance and replies by SMS.

  3. No data

    Offline app

    The installed app still understands the emergency, shows first aid and ranks hospitals from the last saved status. The request is saved and sent automatically when signal returns.

  4. No signal

    Call 112 / 108

    Emergency calls go through on any operator's tower. First-aid steps and the map you already opened stay on the phone.

For ASHA workers, sarpanch & village volunteers

  • Report for someone else – no login, no Aadhaar needed in an emergency.
  • Speak in Hindi; the app reads first aid and directions aloud.
  • Pregnancy emergencies go to 102 Janani Express, everything else to 108, with the accepting hospital already named.
  • The family gets a status-only SMS / link, without medical details.
  • Hospitals keep beds, doctors and blood updated every 108 minutes, so the list is trustworthy even far from the city.

How it works

Emergency → Understand → Match → Verify → Accept → Transport → Confirm

AI understands the situation; a transparent, rule-based engine picks the hospital from staff-verified data; a real person at the hospital accepts.

EmergencySpeak, type or tap. Hindi, English or Hinglish.
UnderstandAI extracts type, severity, red flags and needs.
MatchOnly hospitals with the right service live now.
VerifyBeds and staff verified minutes ago, with the source shown.
AcceptHospital staff accept and assign a bay.
TransportAmbulance or own vehicle, with navigation.
ConfirmConfirmed destination, family kept informed.
Technology

Every suggested technology, used where it helps

Built for Madhya Pradesh: voice-first, bilingual and dependable on weak networks.

Generative AI

Understands panicked, mixed-language descriptions and writes an SBAR handover note for the ER doctor.

Voice bot

Speak in Hindi or English. Acceptance, safety steps and directions are read aloud.

Computer vision

A photo of the injury or scene adds evidence (bleeding, burns, bites) to the triage.

OCR

Scan an Ayushman / ABHA card or prescription on the phone to pre-fill patient details.

Predictive analytics

Traffic-aware ETA, chance of a free bed on arrival, ER wait and arrival forecasts.

Conversational AI

Asks one question at a time (conscious? breathing?) and re-assesses after each answer.

Mobile & offline

Installable app. Triage and hospital matching still work when the network drops.

“I'm alone” mode

One tap shares location, alerts your trusted contact and requests a hospital and an ambulance.

Two separate portals

One for citizens, one for hospitals, kept private from each other

Separate logins, separate sessions and separate data. Hospitals only ever see a patient's details after the family consents, with ID numbers masked.

Citizen portal

  • Report an emergency and follow it live
  • Nearby hospitals with verified beds & specialists
  • Health records & emergency contacts, stored on your phone only
  • My cases, ambulance help and 108 in one place

Hospital staff portal

  • Accept or decline referrals with an AI pre-arrival note
  • Keep beds, ICU, ventilators and duty roster verified
  • Hospital ID + Staff ID + OTP, role-based access
  • Audit log of every action, arrival forecasts
